# 02 · Create your GitLab account

> ⏱️ **~10 min** · 🎯 **Level: Absolute beginner** · 🧰 **Tool needed: a web browser and your work email**

---

## What you'll do

Create a personal account on GitLab — the same way you'd create an account on any website.

> 🏢 **FINTEC note:** FINTEC's projects live in the `ai-fintec` group on **gitlab.com** (the public GitLab service). If your team lead tells you the company uses a different GitLab address (a private company server), use that address instead — the steps are identical, just at a different URL. Stuck? Email **finther.ai@finther.my**.

## Step by step

### 1. Open the sign-up page

Go to **[https://gitlab.com](https://gitlab.com)** and click **Register** (top-right corner), or go straight to **[https://gitlab.com/users/sign_up](https://gitlab.com/users/sign_up)**.

### 2. Fill in the form

| Field | What to enter | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **First name / Last name** | Your real name — teammates will see it | `Siti` / `Rahman` |
| **Username** | Lowercase, no spaces. This becomes your handle in the whole platform | `siti.rahman` or `srahman` |
| **Email** | **Your FINTEC work email** | `siti@finther.my` |
| **Password** | A strong, unique password — never reuse one from another site | *(use a password manager)* |

> 💡 **Tip:** Choose a professional username. It appears next to every change you make, forever. `siti.rahman` ✅ — `cutie_siti_99` ❌

### 3. Verify your email

GitLab sends a confirmation link to your inbox. Click it. No email after a few minutes? Check your spam folder.

### 4. First-time questions

GitLab may ask a few onboarding questions (role, team size, what you'll use it for). Answer honestly or skip — nothing here is permanent. If asked to create a group or project, **skip for now** — you'll join FINTEC's group instead (see [05 · Create or join a project](05-create-or-join-a-project.md)).

### 5. Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA)

> 💡 **Recommended (this is general good practice, not an official FINTEC policy):**

1. Click your **avatar** (top-right) → **Edit profile**
2. Left menu → **Account** → look for **Two-factor authentication**
3. Follow the steps with an authenticator app (e.g. Google Authenticator, Authy)

You'll scan a QR code once; afterwards you type a 6-digit code at each login. It takes 5 minutes and protects the account that will hold your work.

## 🚫 Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Username has already been taken" | Add your initials or a number: `siti.rahman.fintec` |
| Confirmation email never arrives | Check spam. Still nothing: try registering again, then email **finther.ai@finther.my** |
| You already have a personal GitLab account with your private email | Fine — you can either add your work email to that account (**Edit profile → Emails → Add email**) or make a separate work account. Ask your team lead which they prefer |
| You forgot your password | On the sign-in page click **Reset it** — GitLab emails you a recovery link |

## ✅ Done when…

- [x] You can log in at [gitlab.com](https://gitlab.com) and see your dashboard
- [x] Your profile shows your **real name** and **work email**
